Q: How does the delivery process work?

 Diesel Cube (Bulk Storage)

Q: What is the Diesel Cube?
  A: Diesel Cube is Anytime Diesel's industrial-grade bulk diesel storage solution with integrated IoT
  fuel management, engineered to eliminate theft, spillage, and manual tracking. It provides complete
  visibility, safety, and control over every drop of fuel, 24/7. Recommended for users needing a
  minimum of 2,000 litres/week.

Q: What problems does Diesel Cube solve?
  A: It eliminates fuel theft & pilferage, manual data logging errors, unsafe drum/barrel storage (fire
  risk), dead mileage from fuel station queuing, stock dry-outs, wasted manpower, and lack of
  transparency in fuel consumption.

Q: How does the Diesel Cube work?
  A: 1) Secure Storage in a tamper-proof, fire-safe steel enclosure connected to the cloud 24/7. 2)
  RFID/Badge Access — only pre-authorised personnel can dispense fuel. 3) IoT Nozzle Dispenses —
  tracks exact litres per transaction. 4) Dashboard Visibility — all data syncs to the Fuelestic Portal with
  real-time alerts.

Q: What models and capacities are available?
  A: Diesel-Cube IoT (Smart Model): 2,000L and 1,000L (custom order), with real-time IoT sensors,
  RFID dispensing control, 4G/GSM connectivity, mobile app, analytics, alerts, and a 1-month free
  SaaS trial. Diesel-Cube Standard (Non-IoT Model): 2,000L and 1,000L (custom order), with an
  industrial MS steel enclosure, flow meter dispenser, secure locking chamber, and fire insulation.

Q: Who uses the Diesel Cube?
  A: Construction sites, mining operations, hospitals & healthcare, apartments & complexes, logistics &
  fleet operators, RMC & manufacturing units, universities & schools, and remote sites — generally
  businesses needing a minimum of 2,000 litres/week.

Q: What capacities are available for Diesel Pod?

Q: What is excluded from the warranty?

 Installation Process

Q: What are the site readiness requirements?
  A: Structural: minimum 4ft x 4ft area, reinforced concrete base (min 3ft x 3ft), 8ft ceiling height, good
  ventilation. Electrical: dedicated 16A industrial socket, UPS/surge protection recommended, stable
  4G/5G or Wi-Fi signal. Logistics & Safety: clear delivery pathway, crane/forklift access, a CO2/DCP
  fire extinguisher (min 6kg), proper earthing, and required safety signage. A free pre-installation survey
  verifies these requirements.

Q: How long does installation take?
  A: 4 weeks from receipt of the advance payment.

Q: Is delivery and installation free?
  A: Yes, free within 25km of Hyderabad. Beyond 25km, the customer bears freight and offloading
  costs as per actuals. Site readiness and offloading at the site are the customer's responsibility.


 Payment & Commercial Terms

 Other Products

Q: What truck technology is used for deliveries?
  A: Trucks feature an Auto Tank Gauge Sensor (ATG) for 100% accurate dispensing, PESO approval,

  delivery, a 10-metre housing pipe, a 65 km/h speed lock, and GPS tracking.

Q: What are Jerry Cans and their specifications?
  A: PESO-approved, military-grade jerry cans with 20-litre capacity, tamper-proof locks, and
  fireproof/waterproof construction, with a pouring nozzle and leak-proof lock. Delivery can be instant

Q: What is ATD Blue?
  A: ATD Blue is Anytime Diesel's high-quality Diesel Exhaust Fluid (DEF) exceeding ISO 22241

  vehicles.
